# Saxo Bank

Checked on 2026-10-19. Status: **Excluded**. Back to the [index](../README.md).

## Reason for exclusion

Saxo stopped onboarding new clients from Jersey on 1 July 2024. Jersey is on Saxo's list of 41 exit markets. Saxo also said that it would offboard existing Jersey clients by the end of 2024.

- Source: <https://fxnewsgroup.com/forex-news/retail-forex/saxo-bank-informs-its-partners-about-changes-to-country-presence/>
- Source: <https://investingintheweb.com/?p=56349> (review site, updated 2026-10-05)

The research found no exception for corporate or institutional clients. It also found no notice on Saxo's own website. Status: UNCONFIRMED by a primary source.

## Legal entity and regulator (for reference)

- Saxo Bank A/S, Denmark. Regulator: Danish FSA. EU entity, so the Jersey FSC counterparty rule would allow it.
- Other entities: Saxo Bank (Schweiz) AG (FINMA), Saxo Capital Markets UK (FCA).
- The client's country of residence selects the entity. No entity is listed for Jersey. Source: <https://www.home.saxo/accounts/corporate>.

## Next step

Saxo can only return to the list if its entity onboarding team (Entityonboarding@saxobank.com) confirms in writing that it accepts a Jersey corporate. An introducing partner could be an option.
